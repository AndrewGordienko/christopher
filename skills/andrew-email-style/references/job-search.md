## Light resume tailoring

Read the exact job description and preserve the original purple resume as the factual master. For each priority role, map its requirements to demonstrated evidence; then reorder projects and adjust emphasis and accurate terminology. Keep the actual degree, dates, employers, titles, metrics and level of ownership. A missing requirement stays a gap. Do not invent embedded-driver, ROS, Kubernetes, finance, production-scale or other experience from adjacent work.

Prefer a few grounded base variants (planning/robotics/simulation; ML evaluation and efficient deployment; practical software/data systems), with small company-specific changes. Lead with one or two relevant results, not a keyword inventory. Save the exact role source, changes and a keyword-to-evidence map with each variant. Keywords help communicate a real match; they do not establish an ATS pass or guarantee an interview. Render and inspect generated PDFs, and check text extraction and links. Keep variants local until Andrew requests an external action.
