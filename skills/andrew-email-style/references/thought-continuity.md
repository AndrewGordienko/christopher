# Flow and thought continuity

An email should read like one thought developing naturally, not independently optimized biography, company and ask sections.

Before composing, identify the causal chain: what Andrew works on → what he is looking for → why this company or problem caught his attention → why that creates a reason to write → what he is asking. This is a reasoning check, not a mandatory five-part template. Each paragraph should make the next feel expected.

## Connect the work to the company

A specific technical connection can directly follow the biography. Do not force a story about how Andrew found the company or a moral reason he cares. “The failure-detection side of KONUX is close to the work I’m doing now” can be sufficient. Use a bridge only when the connection is otherwise missing; apply [do not manufacture motivation](impact-first-motivation.md).

## Bridge company interest to the ask

The ask should develop from the overlap rather than abruptly changing into commercial language. If needed, add the smallest bridge:

> That problem is close enough to what I've been working on that I was wondering if there might be a project I could take ownership of alongside my current work.

When the connection is already clear, the simpler ending is enough:

> I was wondering if there might be a project I could take ownership of alongside my current work.

For Contract first touches, follow the [current cadence and ask rule](contract-useful-work.md): “a small contract project around that question that I could take ownership of alongside my current work,” with a clear referent. The simpler examples above remain useful in other exploratory modes. Formal scope and payment can follow mutual interest; naming a contract does not authorize a consulting proposal.

## Read-aloud transition test

At every paragraph boundary, ask:

- Why does the next paragraph follow from the previous one?
- Is that connection present in the prose, rather than only in the writer's reasoning?
- Does this sound like one thought developing or like Andrew remembered another talking point?

If the connection is missing, make the smallest natural change. Do not add a bridge when it only repeats an obvious connection or makes a good email longer.

Mentally remove the company and recipient names. If the result is biography → personalized company paragraph → CTA, rewrite the transitions. The target is one coherent reason Andrew decided to email this particular person today. Preserve a current approved draft unless Andrew asks to revise it; this rule is not permission to silently rewrite queued mail.
