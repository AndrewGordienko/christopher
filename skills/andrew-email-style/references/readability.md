# Final readability gate

After grammar and voice checks, ask whether a busy technical person can understand each sentence on the first read. This is a minimal repair pass, not a style rewrite.

For Contract Work, [preserve the original thought](contract-preserve-thought.md) takes precedence over standardizing sentence shape. A developed technical thought or natural bridge is not a defect because it is longer. Identify actual ambiguity before editing, and leave clear, natural sentences alone. Evaluate the whole email's continuity after any local repair.

Flag delayed main verbs, nested abstract ideas, unclear subjects, indirect constructions and sentences that need rereading. Preserve the technical mechanism; remove only the grammatical scaffolding around it. Prefer “I could take ownership of a prototype” to “A prototype is something I could take ownership of.” Do not confuse grammatical correctness with readable English.

Keep Andrew’s current task-specific wording, ordinary cadence and direct technical thought. Do not shorten good sentences for consistency, add a moral preface, or rewrite to manufacture batch variation. Review the complete email after each correction. Bind readability, grammar, voice, factual and external checks to the exact final subject/body; any later edit makes affected checks stale.

The latest text must be visible in the localhost review page before Gmail execution. Verify Gmail matches that exact version, including recipients, attachments and approved future time. Never silently substitute an earlier Gmail draft.
