# Morrow Pilot Email Strategy

Require the [pilot qualifier](../morrow-pilot-qualifier/SKILL.md) to return QUALIFIED with a current evidence score and fingerprint. No writer invocation for POSSIBLE/HOLD.

The first email asks whether one specific cell has the hypothesized recurring human-recovery issue and merits a short technical conversation, or who owns it. Explain Andrew's relevant current role, why that cell is relevant, the recovery hypothesis and the smallest test. Use the current confirmed project name; the skill names do not rename Wapahki or establish formal UofT affiliation. Do not reuse historical age, location, travel, employer or investor facts.

The pilot is one cell: agree passive observation of faults, what operators do and the restored state; identify repeated sequences and intervention time; evaluate/automate one suitable safe case only if justified and separately authorized. Existing logs are possible inputs, not assumed available. State observation and control permissions separately. Never pitch plant-wide downtime analytics or promise physical repairs through software.

Keep customer benefit concrete, such as less time repeatedly resetting this cell. Do not sell the full product, prescribe a consulting engagement, cold-request an LOI, demand machine data, or ask the recipient to teach us about the whole factory. A data-use discussion can follow interest; cross-customer training rights are separate.

Give the [voice writer](../andrew-outbound-voice/SKILL.md) only the compact evidence packet, chosen sender facts, recipient history/voice examples, current draft and qualification fingerprint. No browsing during composition. Then run [thought continuity](../email-thought-continuity-editor/SKILL.md), [cold-email skeptic](../cold-email-skeptic/SKILL.md), and the existing final language/fact/readability checks.
