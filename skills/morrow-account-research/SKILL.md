# Morrow Account Research

Research the operating problem, not the company biography. Follow facility → line → cell → task → robot/OEM/integrator → faults and human interventions → controller/data layer → actual technical owner.

Prefer operator and OEM case studies, integrator records, commissioning documents, facility videos/photos, maintenance information and controls roles that identify the actual system. Verify current operation separately from an announcement. Preserve source URL, original text/quote, event/publication/observation dates, entity and uncertainty locally using the [Andrew research workflow](../andrew-email-style/references/research-workflow.md).

Classify each finding CONFIRMED, LIKELY or UNKNOWN. Keep an inferred recovery mechanism separate from a documented event. Distinguish normal manual work, physical repair/clearing, and a repeatable software/controller sequence. A vendor's generic reset feature does not prove the prospect repeatedly uses it or can expose state.

Resolve the cell owner before a generic executive. A maintenance title alone does not establish responsibility for this facility. Record plausible routing contacts as candidates until their connection is evidenced. Resolve who owns fault/state records and OEM/integrator permissions without treating them as granted access.

Return an evidence packet and the exact remaining questions to [morrow-pilot-qualifier](../morrow-pilot-qualifier/SKILL.md). Do not compose an email. If the cell cannot be identified, return RESEARCH MORE or HOLD; never substitute vague personalization. Do not spend enrichment credits on unqualified facilities merely to fill a batch.
